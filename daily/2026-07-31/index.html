The real-world model: This mirrors how Microsoft's Project Perception (announced July 27, 2026) actually gates its Red/Blue/Green agents. Trivially reversible, contained actions — isolating one machine, rotating a compromised key, blocking a known-bad IP — are candidates for autonomous execution. Actions that are hard to undo or carry real blast radius — patching a live production host, deleting data, running exploits against production, touching systems outside clear scope — stay behind a human sign-off, at least until the system has "earned the right" to more autonomy, in the words of Microsoft's David Weston, corporate VP of AI security.
